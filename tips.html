Health Tips

Simple tips for a healthier you.

Nutrition

Eating healthy food gives you energy to study, play and grow.

  • Eat more fruits and vegetables
  • Drink plenty of water
  • Eat balanced meals
  • Avoid too much junk food

Small choices today, better health tomorrow.

Sleep

Try a consistent bedtime and wind down 30 minutes before sleep.

Exercise

Short daily movement helps concentration and mood.

Hydration

Carry a water bottle and sip throughout the day.

Study Balance

Use focused study blocks and short breaks to stay productive.
